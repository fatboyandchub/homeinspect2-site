Support

Questions, problems or ideas: support@3dlibrary.app. Please say which iPhone you have and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Room scanning

Scanning a room needs the LiDAR scanner found on iPhone Pro models (iPhone 12 Pro and later). On any other iPhone, tap Draw Walls instead and draw the room wall by wall; the floor plan and the wall elevations follow.

iCloud

Your projects sync through your own iCloud. The line at the bottom of the Projects screen says where sync stands. If it says to sign in, open the iPhone's Settings, tap your name at the top and sign in to iCloud; the app needs no account of its own.

Subscription

The subscription is handled by Apple. To change or cancel it, open the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. The first month is free.

Deleting your data

Delete All My Data, in the app's Settings, removes every project from your iPhone and your iCloud. See the privacy policy for what the app stores and where.
